Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay for my booking?

You can reserve your slot online without paying in advance. Payment can be settled in cash or via UPI at the venue reception counter when you arrive.

What if I need to cancel my slot?

You can easily cancel any upcoming reservation directly from your Customer Dashboard before your slot start time without penalty.

Are floodlights included in the turf price?

Yes! High-mast LED floodlight illumination is included for evening and night matches at no extra charge.

Can we book consecutive back-to-back slots?

Yes, you can reserve multiple hours sequentially for tournaments or long match sessions.
