Welcome to Friends Sports Hub Mulayankavu, Kulukkallur's premier sporting complex in Palakkad District, Kerala. Designed specifically for community clubs, football enthusiasts, and family recreation, our complex brings together top-tier athletic infrastructure with comfortable amenities.

  • Footwear: Only turf shoes or rubber molded studs are allowed. Metal studs are strictly prohibited to maintain turf safety.
