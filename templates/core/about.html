A Modern Sports Destination for Every Player

Friends Sports Hub Mulayankavu was created to deliver an unparalleled sporting experience in the Palakkad district. Whether you are playing late-night football under tournament-grade LED floodlights or training for weekend cricket, our synthetic grass pitch offers optimum bounce and reduced injury risk.

In addition to our full-size enclosed turf, we feature a covered, hygienically maintained swimming pool with dedicated change cabins, plus a lively outdoor amusement park for children.
